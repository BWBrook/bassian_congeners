# Decisions

**Last updated:** 2026-07-16

This register records only direction supported by the three concept notes and
the authorised Goal 001 brief. It does not convert field impressions into
findings. Dataset-dependent statements remain unverified until a later,
authorised audit.

## Established direction

### D-001: Comparative scientific centre

The project is a comparative test of repeated congeneric partitioning in
Brush/Common Bronzewings and Black/Grey Currawongs. It must not be reduced to
four independent SDMs or a generic wet/dry habitat comparison.

### D-002: Camera-first, Tasmania-first empirical core

Tasmania and the private camera network are the primary empirical foundation.
Public mainland/Bassian data are secondary and do not displace the camera
network as the main inferential source.

### D-003: Scale and time remain part of the question

The eventual study must distinguish local coexistence, spatial segregation,
and temporal turnover rather than infer mechanism from static range overlap.
The exact supported spatial and temporal units require a data audit.

### D-004: Passive and baited contexts are not naively pooled

The notes distinguish passive main-island deployments from baited King and
Flinders Island deployments. Any later use must preserve that distinction and
justify inclusion; Goal 001 performs no such assessment.

### D-005: Claim discipline

Competition, hybridisation, abundance, and causal mechanism are not established
by the concept notes. Later claims must use uncertainty-aware language and
separate co-occurrence, conditional association, turnover, and replacement
from causal explanations.

### D-006: Goal 001 is foundation and intake only

Goal 001 creates the repo contract, project orientation, decision register,
questionnaire, and durable goal packet. It does not inspect data, implement an
analysis environment, or begin the camera-data audit.

### D-007: Human answers gate Goal 002

The data custodian supplies the immediate gate inputs. Barry and the custodian
must resolve four summary approvals before Goal 002 is framed. Remaining
technical metadata may be completed while Goal 002 is framed, but it becomes
an explicit entry condition and must be resolved before any dataset is opened.

## Provisional hypotheses

- Both congeneric pairs may partition a shared mesic-to-xeric
  ecosystem-structure axis.
- Bronzewings may show finer-scale coexistence, while Currawongs may show
  stronger local dominance, transience, or temporal replacement.
- Site-season detection/non-detection may be an appropriate primary unit, with
  daily occupancy rate as a possible secondary activity proxy.

These are hypotheses to test or revise, not narratives to impose. The audit
must be capable of showing that a shared axis, pair-state analysis, or dynamic
analysis is unsupported.

## Deferred choices

- Canonical data locations, permissions, ownership, privacy, and copy rules.
- Table structure, joins, identifier semantics, and exclusion rules.
- Season definitions, independent-event rules, effort denominators, spatial
  grains, and label-confidence thresholds.
- Whether ambiguous Currawong records support conservative and inclusive
  sensitivity sets.
- Environmental covariates and whether one coherent structural axis exists.
- Analysis language, dependencies, pipeline architecture, data formats, and
  local or remote compute.
- Public mainland/Bassian data acquisition and analysis.
- Model families, manuscript structure, journal, and one-paper versus
  multi-paper strategy.

## Non-goals

- Inspecting, copying, transforming, or summarising a real dataset in Goal 001.
- Creating code, schemas, an R project, renv, targets, data directories, or
  generated outputs in Goal 001.
- Public-data, modelling, GIS, remote/HPC, or manuscript work in Goal 001.
- Treating species-account habitat contrasts as the final contribution.
- Claiming competition from negative association or hybridisation from
  same-frame occurrence.
- Fine-scale diel inference unless timestamp reliability is later established.
